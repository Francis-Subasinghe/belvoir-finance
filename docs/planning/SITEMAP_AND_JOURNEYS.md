## Journey 1: a search visitor learns one thing
1. Search lands the visitor on `/stories/why-profit-isnt-cash`.
2. A one-line summary answers the question straight away, and the body explains it with a diagram.
3. Inline source notes link to primary material (for example, HMRC or ONS).
4. A "Try it" card leads to `/tools/cash-vs-profit` with sensible starting values.
5. The page ends with a related topic and the next story in the reading path.

**Success:** the visitor scrolls the story, clicks a source or the tool, and then visits one related page.

## Journey 2: a returning reader subscribes
1. The visitor opens the Home page and reads the featured "What changed" briefing.
2. The briefing shows the original source, its date, and Belvoir's take on why it matters.
3. A newsletter call to action explains what the reader gets and how often it arrives.
4. Signup asks for an email address, explicit consent and a bot check. A confirmation email follows (double opt-in).
5. The confirmed state links to the editorial standards and the unsubscribe policy.

**Success:** the reader confirms the opt-in, and nothing beyond the email address and consent record is stored.
